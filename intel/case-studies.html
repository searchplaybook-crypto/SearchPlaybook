Case studies

Real South African businesses we've worked with: what we found, and what we built.

Case 01

East Rand, Gauteng

VH1 Value Home

Website-to-WhatsApp catalogue

What we built

  • A full product catalogue website: 25+ products across three categories, in one fast, lightweight file.
  • A WhatsApp button on the catalogue, so customers ask about or order a product straight from their phone.
  • A share widget, so customers can forward a product to friends and family in a tap.
  • Images served from a content delivery network (ImageKit), so pages load quickly on mobile data.

Why it matters

This is the clearest example of our website-to-WhatsApp model: no checkout to maintain, no monthly platform fee, and every enquiry lands in the WhatsApp chat the business already uses.

Case 03

Eastgate and Nelson Mandela Square, Sandton

X-Seption

Luxury menswearGoogle Business Profile

What we found

3,418 → 3Google Business Profile views that turned into WhatsApp chats over six months: a 99.1% drop-off.

People were finding the stores on Google, but almost none of them were starting a conversation.

What we built

  • A Google Business Profile audit and a competitor analysis.
  • WhatsApp message sequences for enquiries.
  • Ongoing management of both stores' Google Business Profiles, with owner updates posted for them.
  • A search-friendly lookbook website for their new campaign (in progress).
Case 04

Newclare, Johannesburg

Family Kitchen

Google Business Profile reportIn-store tools

What we found

A 60-day Google Business Profile performance report, with findings ranked by severity and a fix checklist. It also surfaced a problem no dashboard shows: orders taking 50+ minutes because of miscommunication between the cashier and the kitchen.

What we built

  • A colour-coded staff reference card for the till: four A3 sheets covering 100+ menu items.
  • A wall menu display.
  • An interactive game page with an embedded YouTube Short.

Figures come from each client's own Google Business Profile data and our audit reports. Shared with our clients' permission.
