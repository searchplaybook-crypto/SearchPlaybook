How to rank in “near me” searches

Google matches local buyers to businesses using relevance, distance and prominence. You cannot pay for a better ranking, but you control more of it than you think.

Why it matters

Visibility

Why it matters

People search for “near me” when they are ready to buy, often from a phone. Appearing there puts you in front of customers who are already close by.

Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence. Two of those three are in your hands.

Steps

Visibility

What to do

Six things to do, in order

  1. Get verified. A verified profile is more likely to appear in search results. See the Google Business Profile guide.
  2. Fill in every detail. Use your full address if customers visit, regular and special opening hours, and attributes such as parking or Wi-Fi. Wrong details can stop you appearing for the right searches.
  3. Choose the category that best describes what you sell. It is how Google matches you to what people search for.
  4. Add photos and videos of what you offer, so people can see it before they visit.
  5. Ask happy customers for reviews, and reply to every review. Reviews, ratings and links from other websites all add to how well known you are.
  6. Check your address is exactly right. Distance is the one factor you cannot change, because Google measures it from where the customer is to where you are.
Avoid

Visibility

Common mistakes

  • Choosing a broad category such as “Store” when a more exact one exists.
  • Leaving opening hours out of date after load shedding or holiday changes.
  • Asking for reviews only once, then never replying to the ones you get.
  • Trying to buy or fake reviews, which puts the whole profile at risk.
Check

Visibility

Quick check

You are done when:

  • The profile is verified and the category matches what you actually sell.
  • Hours, address and phone number are correct today.
  • Your last five reviews all have a reply.
