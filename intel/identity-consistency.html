Identity consistency checklist

Google trusts details that match everywhere, and so do customers. Check these in order.

Why it matters

Privacy

Why it matters

When your name, address or phone number differ between your website, your Google profile and your social pages, both people and search engines have to guess which one is right.

A wrong or old phone number also sends real customers to the wrong place. Matching details are cheap to keep and costly to repair.

Steps

Privacy

What to do

Five steps

  1. Write your master copy once. Your trading name, your address and your phone number in +27 format, exactly as they should appear.
  2. Check the main places. Your Google Business Profile, your website (contact page and footer), Facebook and other social pages, review sites and business directories.
  3. Fix the mistakes that change the meaning. A wrong or old phone number, an old address, or a different business name. Small formatting differences, such as “St” and “Street”, are generally fine.
  4. Start with the most visible listings. Errors are quick to create and slow to fix, so fix what customers see first.
  5. Check again after any change. A move, a new number or a new name means going through the list once more.
Avoid

Privacy

Common mistakes

  • Using a personal cellphone number on one profile and the business line on another.
  • Forgetting an old directory listing that still shows your previous address.
  • Changing the trading name on one platform only.
Check

Privacy

Quick check

You are done when:

  • You have one saved master copy of your name, address and +27 number.
  • Google, your website and your social pages all show that exact copy.
  • Old listings with a wrong number or address are fixed or removed.
