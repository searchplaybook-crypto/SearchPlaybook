SA Youth.mobi

Free · no data
  • Jobs, learnerships, internships, volunteering and skills programmes for young people.
  • Free to use with no data needed, so you can even search on a borrowed phone.
  • Run by Harambee Youth Employment Accelerator, as part of the Presidential Youth Employment Intervention.

Google Skillshop

Free
  • Free Google Ads and Google Analytics certifications. You only need a Google account.
  • Most certifications last a year, then you retake the exam to stay certified.
  • Useful for digital marketing, admin and small-business support roles.
