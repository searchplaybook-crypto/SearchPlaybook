Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information, in plain language.

3. Help with intimate images (NCII)

  • Never send us the images themselves. Send links to where they appear, or describe where you found them.
  • NCII requests go to searchplaybook@proton.me, an encrypted mailbox with Proton, a Swiss company. Only Llewellyn O'Shea reads them.
  • If the person in the images is under 18, report it to the South African Police Service on 10111.
  • Our NCII protection guide explains your options and where to get help.

4. Cookies

Our Home page, Intel Hub and its guides, and this page don't use analytics or advertising cookies.

Our Solutions and Collective pages load Google Analytics, which measures how the pages are used, and Google AdSense, which shows ads. Both set cookies, and Google may use AdSense cookies to show you ads based on your visits to other websites.

You can control this:

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • Manage how Google personalises ads in Google's My Ad Center.
  • Install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

5. Who we share it with

We never sell or rent your personal information. We use these service providers to run the site and do our work:

  • Google: analytics and ads on two pages, web fonts, and the Google Business Profile work you ask us to do
  • Meta: WhatsApp, where most conversations with us happen
  • GitHub: hosts this website and the websites we build
  • ImageKit and our image hosts: deliver the site's images and videos
  • Proton: the encrypted email service that receives privacy and NCII requests
  • domains.co.za: hosts our business email

We'll also share information if the law requires us to, for example under a court order.

6. Information sent outside South Africa

Several of these providers store information outside South Africa, including in the United States, Europe, Switzerland and India. We use established providers that are subject to data-protection laws or binding agreements that give protection similar to POPIA.

8. Your rights

Under POPIA you can:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or update it
  • Ask us to delete it, where the law doesn't require us to keep it
  • Object to us using it, including for direct marketing
  • Withdraw consent you've given us
  • Complain to the Information Regulator

To use any of these rights, email searchplaybook@proton.me or message us on WhatsApp. We'll reply within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm who you are first, so we don't give your information to someone else.

If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Regulator using Form 5 on its POPIA forms page, or email enquiries@inforegulator.org.za.

9. Marketing messages

We only send you marketing messages if you've asked to hear from us. Reply STOP at any time and we'll stop.

10. Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including limiting access to what each job needs. No system is perfectly secure. If a security breach affects your personal information, we'll tell you and the Information Regulator, as POPIA requires.

11. Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We don't knowingly collect personal information about children, except where a parent or guardian asks us for help.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date below.
