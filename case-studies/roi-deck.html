In plain English

1. One setup, no monthly fees. In June 2024 the business’s Google Business Profile and search visibility were set up as a Digital Birth Certificate. The cost was R4,500, split over two years. There is no subscription and no paid advertising.

2. It paid for itself early. The setup covered its cost after about 50 orders, in under two months.

3. 418 orders to date. From June 2024 to November 2026, the digital channels brought in an estimated 418 orders, with about R33,120 profit after the R4,500 setup cost.

4. Each order costs far less than advertising. The setup works out to R10.77 per order. A paid search ad in this market costs about R20 per order, so this is about 46% cheaper.

Order figures are estimates. The business records orders by hand, so these numbers are worked out from those records and the Google and WhatsApp figures.

What is holding growth back

Customers are finding the business. The visibility side is healthy. The limit is what happens after the order: waiting times stretched from about 15 minutes to more than 50 minutes at busy times. Long waits mean fewer customers come back. With two new staff now in place, there is room to take more orders if waiting times come down.

WhatsApp Business line

The WhatsApp Business line started in late September 2026. In October and November it handled about 90 chats a month, an estimated 13 to 22 orders a month from WhatsApp alone. Orders are recorded by hand, so this is an estimate, not a till count.

Accounts and data

How the setup keeps the business in control.

The business owns the accounts. The Google Business Profile and WhatsApp line belong to the business. We Must Create is added as a manager. Passwords are never shared.
Customer details stay with the business. Chats and orders sit in Google and WhatsApp, under the business's own login. They are not sold or shared.
Access can be removed at any time. The owner can remove a manager from the profile in a few taps.

Next steps

None of these need new advertising spend.
